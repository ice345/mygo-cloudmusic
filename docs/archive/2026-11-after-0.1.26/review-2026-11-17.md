# Review of mygo-cloudmusic, 2026-11-17

Reviewed `main` at `7c8f425`. This is a snapshot: what was fixed since, and what is still open, is in [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../roadmap.md).

**How it was checked.** Three reviewers looked for defects: the non-UI packages, `internal/app`, and the repository's tests, build and release. Each finding then went to a second reviewer whose job was to refute it, re-reading the code and, where possible, writing a throwaway repro test in its own worktree. None of the 41 findings was refuted; several were downgraded. Separately, one reviewer audited the plan in this folder against the commits; one evaluated the lyric display and rendered extra `NM_FRAMES` frames; one researched both films' visual language from Japanese and English sources; one designed a style and prototyped its tones with the real engine; and a last one tried to break that design. The two release blockers, the Korean-spacing bug and the claims about the release secret and the speech libraries were checked again by hand.

## 0. In short

The plan in this folder has been built. The 12 commits after it implement nearly all of it, including 青鸟, a style after 『リズと青い鳥』. But none of it had been pushed or released, the fork could not build a release, and the new code had a release-blocking notch regression and several defects in how lyrics are shown. Fix those before adding a style after 『響け！ユーフォニアム』.

## 1. Defects and risks

### P0: blocks the next release

| Issue | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **The notch card and the first-run greeting can't be used** (regression in `c49756f`). The pointer counted as inside only up to 6 pt below the housing, even with the card open. Moving down to the controls let clicks through to the app underneath, and the card closed about 300 ms later. A new listener couldn't click 开启 on the greeting. | `internal/app/ui_notch.go:334-338` | Hit area `bottom := n.screenY + h + slack` with `h` from `target()`, cut to `n.h0` only while lyrics hang under the housing. A test that the open card keeps the pointer. |
| **The fork can't build a release.** `release.yml` needs `MYGO_UPDATER_PRIVATE_KEY`, and the repository had no secrets, so every build job would fail after the draft was made. `main` was 12 commits ahead of `origin/main`, with no tags or releases; the repository was private, so the update feed answered 404. | `.github/workflows/release.yml:65` | Add the secret and a step that fails without it. Push `main`. Make the repository public. |

### P1: wrong in normal use

- **A network error skipped the whole queue.** Every load error, not only "can't play", moved on to the next track. Starting offline walked the restored queue, stopped at 0:00 on another track, and saved that over the session. `internal/player/player.go:643`. Skip only what the track itself can't play; stay on transport errors.
- **Podcast captions couldn't install.** The speech libraries downloaded from upstream's release, which is gone (upstream is archived), and the listener was told to check their network. `internal/speech/natives.go:9`. `scripts/sherpa/main.go:43` hard-coded upstream, so following `RELEASING.md` regenerated the same URL.
- **Word-timed lyrics lose words in parentheses.** "(oh" vanishes, and "(Ah)" goes entirely: the word pattern `[^(]*` stops at the first `(`. `internal/lyrics/lyrics.go:75`. Split at the timing tags instead.
- **Lines without word timing lose the space after Han and Hangul once lit.** Korean shows as 너의모든걸원해, and 云阶 splits Korean words. `SpreadWords` writes a space only after Latin text (`lyrics.go:401-405`); `TestSpreadWords` expects this.
- **The line before comes back after a long break.** 青鸟 draws it again at about 30% for 250 ms (`ui_stage_bird.go:105`, the `current &&` in the long-break test). 流光, 云阶 and 倾诉 bring back the old translation for 120 ms and the old words for 400 ms (`ui_stage.go:139-144`, `:174-177`).
- **The lyrics under the notch appear late after an intro and stay through the outro.** Nothing schedules a build for the first line or the last line's end. `ui_notch.go:628`.
- **青鸟's translation arrives before its line has formed, and sits left of centre**, so it reads as misaligned rather than answering from the right. The plan contradicts itself here (`next.md` §5.3 against §5.4).

### P2

- **Skipping didn't cancel a download waiting for bytes**, and streams use `http.DefaultClient`, with no timeout: stalled connections, goroutines and `.part` files outlive the track. `player.go:560`, `stream.go:100`.
- **Two LRC lines at one time** (a translation uploaded as its own line): the original is never current, the translation shows as the sung line in Japanese forms, and the stage's keys collide (MyGo panics on that in tests and shares state in the app). `lyrics.go:145`, `ui_stage.go:280`.
- **A failed lyrics request shows 「纯音乐，请欣赏」** and is never retried; cached songs played offline all read as instrumental. `player.go:604`.
- **A cover that fails to load once stays blank** for the session. `artwork.go:129`.
- **`settings.json` is written in place, with errors ignored**: a crash mid-write resets every setting for good. `settings.go:144`. Write and rename, as `saveSession` does.
- **(Plausible, not demonstrated)** `SongURL` ignored NetEase's code, so a refusal such as -460 read as "needs VIP" and was skipped. `netease.go:432`.
- **The notch's levels repaint at the display's full rate**, 120 Hz on ProMotion, through all of playback. `ui_notch.go:513`, `ui_pages.go:449`. `AnimationFrameRate(30)`.
- **In 歌词, the Chinese translation draws in Japanese glyph forms**: `.Lang(l.Lang)` is on the row (`ui_nowplaying.go:554`), and the translation inherits it.
- **Tests and CI.** `go test ./internal/...` had failed on Linux and Windows since `a44e5d4` (the two notch greeting tests, which also fail on a Mac running Atoll). Nothing ran tests on push, pull request or release. `-race` reports 5 races, all from test seams read inside goroutines, and the app's suite times out under `-race`.

### P3 and structure

- **Lyrics parsing.** A blank timed line doesn't end the line before it, which stays lit for up to 6 s. Time tags match anywhere in a line, and `[offset:]` is ignored. Japanese is detected by counting kana lines, which misfires on mostly-English J-pop. Podcast captions don't end a sentence at an ASCII `.`. Transcript saves race on one `.tmp` file.
- **Style plumbing.** Stage styles are dispatched by string literals in 6 places across 3 files. An unknown id draws as 流光, and `stage_test.go:65` lists the styles by hand.
- **Large state.** The `app` struct has about 95 fields, and `newTestApp` copies `Run`'s constructor and has drifted from it. `ui_notch.go` is 790 lines whose state is 8 interacting booleans, with branches only tests take.
- **Duplication.** `lyrics.Source` repeats `netease.LyricTexts` field for field. Caption sizes and opacities are literals in 4 files. 青鸟's timing is written twice.
- **Tooling.** `debug_shots.go` ships in release builds and panics off macOS with `NM_SHOTS` set. The default test run makes about 141 HTTPS requests to NetEase. Three frame tests call NetEase without the `LIVE` gate.
- **Dependencies and site.** MyGo is pinned to an untagged commit; tagged releases reach v0.9.2. The README says Go 1.27+, while `go.mod` needs 1.27.3. The website's build fetches upstream's latest release, now 404.
- **Hygiene.** `.DS_Store` files are tracked; `wip/reading-caption` is superseded; frame folders aren't ignored.

## 2. The plan

**Delivered.** WP1 to WP6 and WP7's single lyric request, in about 2 h 45 m on the plan's day, the commit subjects copied from the guide. The tests it asked for exist and passed 5 of 5. WP0 was partly done: the repository name and default branch were settled, but there was no signing secret, no push and no release. The work meant for 0.1.27 and 0.1.28 sat in one unreleased batch.

**Strengths.** Acceptance criteria are numbers a test can check; every step names its files, functions and tests; the claims about the film cite sources; the order was right and was followed.

**Weaknesses.**

- **Contradictions**, mostly where the guide restates `next.md` with small changes: the attachment window (500 against 200 ms), the Japanese-song rule ("or" against "max"), `Lang` on the row against "not on readings", when 青鸟's answer arrives, and decisions settled in one file but open in the other.
- **Length.** 914 dense lines that assume prior context ("the tree", "the previous draft").
- **Subjective gates with no pass or fail**: "review frames with the owner", "look in the app", the manual Atoll check. None left a trace, and the defects above are what they were meant to catch.
- **Gaps**: CI; the fork's release prerequisites (signing key, speech libraries, website); Windows and Linux parity (the notch work is macOS only); accessibility (青鸟's captions fail WCAG AA, and the "disabled" notch-lyrics control still takes input); error states; a policy for upgrading MyGo now that upstream is archived.

**Recommendation.** Archive both files (this folder) and replace them with short living docs: a one-page `roadmap.md`, `lyrics.md` (the measured NetEase rules, as built), `stage.md` (conventions and tokens), `notch.md` (with a manual checklist for a Mac), and a `CONTRIBUTING.md` from the guide's §0-2 and §5. Put acceptance criteria in tests rather than prose.

## 3. The lyric display

**Sound.** `internal/lyrics` is pure and tested without views. One pair of switches (`lyricReading` and `lyricTranslation`, `ui_nowplaying.go:318/326`) serves every surface. The stage is a function of where playback is, so a seek lands on the frame playing would show. Frame budgets live in `perf_test.go`.

**Weak.** Typography is hard-coded per surface, which is why there are three hierarchies (§4). Adding a style is costly: a style that owns the stage, as 青鸟 does, must redo the empty and loading states, breaks, the intro and outro, its own frames and its own captions, and thread a backdrop `glass bool` through five functions; sparks, hot comments and the foot band are switched by comparing ids. About 1-2 days for a style that only draws lines and keeps the foot band, 3-5 for one that owns the stage. Refactor first: a `stageStyle` struct holding `sparks`, `hotComments`, a backdrop tone, `lead` and the drawing function, read by the menu, `stage()` and Now Playing.

## 4. Translations and readings

**Today** the order is the same everywhere (reading above translation), but the weight isn't.

| Surface | Reading | Translation | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| 歌词 list | 17/500, 55% | 17/600, 70% | Both rows inherit the line's `ja`/`ko`, so the Chinese translation draws in Japanese forms |
| Foot band (流光, 云阶, 倾诉) | Whichever row is first is 18/600/72% | The second row is ×0.88 size, ×0.82 opacity | Turning on 音译 shrinks and dims the translation |
| 青鸟 | 0.32× the line, 46%, sans | 0.38×, 62%, serif, 14% in from the line's left | Left of centre; about 3.0:1 and 3.7:1 contrast; the reading sits against the translation (6 px) rather than the line (25 px); its width follows the line's, so a one-character line stacks 回 / 响; breaks inside a word (続 / いていく) |
| Notch | Takes the one extra row | Dropped when both are on | The meaning is lost; the word being sung can sit behind the ellipsis |

**Recommended rules for every surface:**

1. **Style by role, never by what else is on.** In the foot band, keep the translation at 18/600/72% always and give the reading the quieter step. The band grows up from the bottom, so turning 音译 on then neither moves nor restyles the translation. This departs from Folia's "the first row is louder"; it is the owner's call.
2. **Grouping.** The reading binds tight to the line (a gap of about 0.08-0.1 of the line's size); the translation is its own group (about 0.35).
3. **The translation carries the meaning for these listeners**: never smaller or fainter than the reading, never below 18 px on the stage.
4. **The reading is Latin**: the system sans, no `Lang`, at `max(15, 0.34 × line)`. Latin's small x-height makes it look about 2.4 times smaller than CJK at one size.
5. **`Lang` goes on the line's own text only.** The translation is `zh-Hans`.
6. **Both captions reach 4.5:1** against the lightest backdrop they can sit on.
7. **Wrapping.** The translation's width doesn't depend on the line's. Prefer two balanced rows broken at punctuation. Never break between a kanji and its okurigana (続|い, 思い|出す). Break Korean only at spaces. MyGo exposes no control over line breaks (`NoBreakWords` is internal), so this needs a breaker in the app; BudouX (Apache-2.0) is worth a look.
8. **Notch.** Two rows: the translation if on, else the reading. Three rows with both on: the line, the reading, the translation, dropping the line before. Replace the end ellipsis with a window that follows the lit word.

## 5. A style after 『リズと青い鳥』 and 『響け！ユーフォニアム』

**Feasible: yes.** MyGo has what it needs: blur, opacity, transforms, gradients in Oklab, font family lists, letter spacing, `Lang`, text measurement, colour per span, and Reduce Motion. Missing, with ways round: radial gradients (a falloff bitmap, as the prototype did), glow on text (the app already fakes it with a blurred copy), and control of line breaks (in the app).

**What the research found.** Many Japanese pages could only be read through search summaries or translations; quotes from those are marked † in the sources below and should be checked before they are quoted again.

- **Liz.** Yamada asked for the colour of thick, slightly green glass [1†]. The art director 篠原 aimed for 「ガラスを覗いたような透明感」 [4†] and, against the strong backgrounds of the series, lowered the saturation, went high-key and added a little blue [6†]. The camera "peeks" as if through a telephoto lens [2†, 3]; Yamada values 間, because their feelings break when put into words [9]. Ushio scored it from a bystander's view: footsteps as music, decalcomania that looks alike but is never symmetric, and 互いに素, neighbours drifting apart [7, 10].
- **Euphonium.** High-contrast, "powerful" backgrounds and backlit compositions [32†]; "a world with shallow depth of field" [31†, a fan translation]; critics note flare and bokeh [36, 37†]. Golden hour or dusk as a palette rule has no production source.
- **Corrections to `next.md` §5.1.** "Thick, slightly green glass" is sourced, but to Yamada, not the colour designer [1†]. "Drawing the girls' sighs" is a MANTANWEB headline [1†]. The stairs metaphor has no source.

**青鸟 against the research.** Its direction is right: stillness, the whole line condensing, a hairline in breaks, no sparks, the translation answering as a second voice. It misses on the palette (a lifted mid-grey, not pale, which white text rules out and the spec should say; grey covers stay neutral; red covers turn mauve; the hue jumps at 12°), on the answer's timing and place, on contrast, and on fonts (no Korean serif).

**Recommendation: a pair of styles, not one blend.** The sources oppose each other on nearly every axis: pale, cool, still and private against high-contrast, warm, moving and public. A blended dusk prototype came out as mud. If exactly one new style is wanted, the coherent blend is "window light": the glass field, warm light from one side, rack focus and the answer, about a day on top of the 青鸟 fixes.

- **Step 0, the foundation (2-3 days):** the style registry, a backdrop tone enum, the line breaker, the Korean-spacing fix.
- **Step 1, 青鸟 v2 (2.5-3 days):** one glass for every cover (hue about 186°, low saturation, the cover only as a light-and-dark wash; per-cover hue shifts were invisible in the prototype anyway, ΔE about 1.3). Tokens: reading `#EEF4F6` at 0.82 and translation `#F3EEDF` at 0.88, at least 4.85:1. The answer flush with the line's right edge, or a column to its right when wider. A constant delay of about 0.4 s after the first word, leaving 0.4 s later: waiting until the line has formed would halve the translation's reading time on 3 s lines. A Korean serif.
- **Step 2, the Euphonium sibling (4-6 days),** from the sourced cues only: high contrast and a backlight bloom behind the line; rack focus as each line comes in; one brass gold on the word being sung, on word-timed lines only; the translation **in unison**, on the line's axis. That last is the one deliberate difference between the two: Liz's parts "don't mesh", so 青鸟 answers late and off-axis, while an ensemble plays together. Hot comments stay, which suits its public tone. A name like 和鸣 fits better than the prototype's 余晖 ("afterglow"), which is elegiac. Paint any bokeh into the backdrop's canvas so it crossfades with it.
- **Effort:** 7.5-9.5 days by the designer's estimate; 10-13 by the critic's, which is likelier.
- **Never:** characters, uniforms, the school or its places; title logos or key-visual compositions; birds, feathers, blue or yellow flowers; the "disjoint → joint" strike-through; particular shots; the music or samples of it; the works' titles in labels or release notes. 青鸟 is a generic word (Maeterlinck's).

## 6. Order

1. **Before any release:** fix the notch's hit area; add the signing secret and push `main`; make the repository public; add CI and make the notch tests portable; stay put on network errors; host the speech libraries on the fork.
2. **Lyrics:** parentheses in word-timed lyrics, Korean spacing, lines at one time, `Lang` on the list's rows, an error state instead of 纯音乐, the line coming back after breaks, the notch strip's timing. Then cut 0.1.27.
3. The typography rules, 青鸟 v2 and the registry refactor.
4. The Euphonium sibling, with frames looked at on real covers.
5. The structure and P3 items.
